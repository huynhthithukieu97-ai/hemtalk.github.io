Why Hẻm Talk

Three house rules

Đời thật

Real life first

We learn what people say in the hẻm, at the market and at the office. Not stiff lines from an old textbook.

Thanh trước

Tone before grammar

Wrong grammar, people still understand. Wrong tone, you said another word. We fix your tones from day one, one person at a time.

Dám nói

Confidence first

Every class ends with role-play. You leave having actually spoken, not just listened.

I grew up in Nha Trang and live in Saigon. By day I'm a Project Manager at a global marketing agency, working with colleagues from all over the world. On the side, I teach Vietnamese, because nothing beats the moment someone orders their first cà phê sữa đá without switching to English.

Questions

Hỏi nhanh, đáp gọn

Why the Saigon accent?

It is what you hear every day here. You speak the Saigon way and write with standard spelling, so you are understood anywhere in Vietnam.

I've lived here for years. Too late?

No. You already know more than you think. We start from the words you've picked up and fix the tones first.

Can my family or colleagues join?

Yes. Groups of 2–4 people who live or work together learn fastest, because you practise together every day.

What do I need?

Your own phone or laptop, headphones and a quiet spot. One person per device, so Kiều can hear and correct each of you.

What if I miss a lesson?

You keep the slides and the practice app for every lesson. Ask about rescheduling when you book.

Do you teach 1-on-1?

Yes, online. Message Kiều with your goals and available evenings.
